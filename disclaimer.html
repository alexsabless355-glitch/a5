Artisanal Disclosures & Leathercraft Disclaimer

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Maison Governance Standards

1. Handcrafted Artisanal Tolerances and Unique Character

All handbags, clutches, and leather goods crafted by CarryCouture are individual works of haute maroquinerie executed by hand. Because our artisans do not employ automated stamping dies or computerized sewing robots, subtle millimeter micro-variations in saddle stitch slant, edge paint bevel thickness, and hand-chiseled engraving are intentional signatures of human mastery rather than manufacturing flaws.

2. Organic Leather Behavior and Patina Evolution

Full-grain French leathers and vegetable-tanned hides are natural biological materials that breathe and respond to their environment. Patina maturation rates, surface color deepening, and natural leather softening depend on individual user handling, climate exposure, and atmospheric humidity. We cannot guarantee identical patina development timelines across different environments.

3. Digital Simulator and Craft Studio Approximations

The dermal tensile metrics, patina timelines, and artisan assembly hours calculated by our interactive Craft Studio workbench represent idealized mathematical models based on standard French tannery specifications. Actual tensile density and handcraft hours vary depending on hide batch thickness and bespoke customization requests.

4. Non-Affiliation and Editorial Independence

Technical analyses published in our Journal de Maroquinerie regarding classical saddlery techniques (such as Point Sellier, Fil Au Chinois linen, and French boxcalf tanneries) are educational and historical in nature. CarryCouture operates as an independent private atelier and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any external commercial fashion conglomerate.

5. Atelier Salon Governance Contact

If you have questions regarding our craftsmanship disclosures, material provenance certifications, or atelier terms, please contact our salon stewardship team at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact us by telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
