Digital Stewardship

Cookie Policy & Digital Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Maison Privacy Standards

1. Principles of Digital Restraint and Luxury Discretion

Maison CarryCouture upholds an unwavering commitment to digital elegance, distraction-free browsing, and client data privacy. We believe your exploration of our haute maroquinerie archives should remain private and unburdened by invasive third-party ad retargeting trackers. This Cookie Policy transparently details our use of essential session storage and local preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Session Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the secure operation, cryptographic verification, and cryptographic rendering stability of our digital maison. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session states, verify client form submissions, defend against cross-site request forgery attacks, and ensure stable progress loading across journal articles. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without compromising platform security.

3. Local Storage for Palette and Reading Preferences

To ensure optimal visual comfort during extended reading of our technical treatises, CarryCouture utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your preferred interface theme (Haute Noir vs. Atelier Light Palette). This configuration data resides strictly within your device browser sandbox and is never transmitted to commercial marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We deploy privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to assess aggregate website loading latencies, feature engagement, and display dimensions. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring no individual client identity or salon appointment history is ever linked to browsing patterns.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

Clients maintain complete freedom to review, disable, or delete stored cookies and local storage tokens at any time through web browser preferences (supported in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that clearing essential storage may reset your custom theme preferences.

6. Governance Inquiries and Data Stewardship Contact

For questions regarding our cookie protocols or to discuss our digital privacy governance, please contact our data stewardship desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone us at +1-888-777-5845.
