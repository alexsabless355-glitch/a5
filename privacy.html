Privacy Policy & Client Data Confidentiality

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Maison Governance Standards

1. Commitment to Client Discretion and Data Privacy

At Maison CarryCouture, we hold the privacy, personal discretion, and transaction confidentiality of our private salon clients, collectors, and digital visitors in the highest regard. We operate under strict data minimization principles, adhering fully to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and international privacy governance frameworks to ensure your bespoke commission records and personal identity remain secure.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To coordinate private atelier appointments, execute bespoke leathercraft commissions, and maintain lifetime restoration records, we collect client contact details (name, private email, telephone number, and physical salon delivery addresses). Technical browsing telemetry, including pseudonymized IP addresses and device browser parameters, is gathered exclusively to maintain encrypted digital connections and optimize website rendering speed.

3. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Information Processing

Personal information is processed strictly to fulfill bespoke commission contracts, schedule private Mercer Street salon consultations, issue official certificate-of-provenance records, and facilitate secure courier shipping of finished handbags. We never process client data for automated behavioral profiling or intrusive commercial marketing campaigns without your explicit prior consent.

4. Absolute Prohibition on Commercial Data Sale & Third-Party Disclosure

CarryCouture enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose client records or purchase histories to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing syndicates. Data is shared exclusively with vetted operational partners—such as armored logistics couriers and SOC 2 certified cloud infrastructure hosts—under strict non-disclosure covenants.

5. Cryptographic Security Protocols and Archival Retention

All digital communications, appointment requests, and client ledger entries are shielded using modern TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Bespoke commission provenance records are retained securely in our archival vaults to ensure lifelong verification for our clients and their designated heirs.

6. Universal Client Rights and Privacy Officer Contact

Clients maintain complete legal rights to inspect, update, export, or request the erasure of their personal information at any time. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our data stewardship architecture, please contact our Data Governance Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our salon desk at +1-888-777-5845.
