2. Bespoke Commission Roster, Deposits & Creation Schedules

Because each CarryCouture handbag is handcrafted by a single master artisan utilizing two-needle Point Sellier saddle stitching, production volumes are strictly limited. Bespoke commissions require an initial 50% non-refundable deposit upon final hide selection to secure tannery allocations and artisan scheduling. Estimated creation lead times range from 8 to 14 weeks depending on leather rarity and guilloché engraving complexity.

3. Intellectual Property Rights & Architectural Design Ownership

Reproduction, reverse-engineering, or unauthorized commercial copying is strictly prohibited.

4. Organic Material Character and Natural Leather Variations

Full-grain French boxcalf, Togo, and Barenia leathers are pure organic materials. Natural variations in dermal grain, subtle neck wrinkles, and organic vein patterns are authentic hallmarks of uncorrected premium hides and do not constitute structural flaws. Each handcrafted piece is inherently unique.

5. Lifetime Atelier Spa Warranty and Service Limitations

Every authentic CarryCouture creation includes lifetime access to our restoration spa for edge re-glazing, ultrasonic hardware polishing, and seam re-waxing. The warranty covers authentic craftsmanship and material integrity under ordinary luxury usage, but excludes damage resulting from chemical solvent spills, animal bites, or unauthorized third-party cobbler alterations.
